Ported into this project: blocks/todo-tower/Tower.tsx (the Todo Tower block,
which inlines lab/rigid.ts and lab/spring), at commit
84dfcee170171257a28631402d37ff34900bc773.

What was ported: the 2D rigid-body solver (separating-axis collision with
clipped contact points, sequential impulses with friction and warm starting),
the "a hand that cannot crush" steering model, the rider/grip rules for a
carried stack, and the settle-then-park frame loop. The C# port lives in
desktop/Tower.cs (physics) and desktop/TodoTower.cs (the island's view).
